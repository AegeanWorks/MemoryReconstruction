# Asset Manifest

The art, data and shaders in `assets/` are original AegeanWorks work, most of it produced by the
generator scripts in `scripts/`. They are offered under AGL-1.1 as part of the game: they may be
used to play the game and in free mods, and may not be extracted or redistributed on their own
without a written asset grant. No third-party art, audio or font asset ships.

| Path or pattern | Type | Rights holder | License | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `assets/sprites/`, `assets/banner.png` | sprites and banner | AegeanWorks | AGL-1.1 | generated by `scripts/generate_*.py` |
| `assets/graphs/`, `assets/shaders/` | particle, tile and UI graphs; shaders | AegeanWorks | AGL-1.1 | original |
| `assets/data/` | level and balance data | AegeanWorks | AGL-1.1 | original |
